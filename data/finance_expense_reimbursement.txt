Finance: Business travel expense reimbursement. Employees may reclaim reasonable taxi or cab fares incurred to attend a client meeting when the travel has a documented business purpose and manager approval. Upload the receipt, travel date, client or project code, and purpose in the Expense Portal within 15 calendar days of the journey. Finance reviews the claim and pays approved amounts in the next reimbursement cycle. Personal rides and ordinary home-to-office commuting are not eligible. For a missing receipt, request guidance from Finance rather than inventing supporting evidence.
