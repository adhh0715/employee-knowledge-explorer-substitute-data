Security: Suspicious links and data protection. If you accidentally click a suspicious link, stop interacting with the page, do not enter credentials, and report the incident immediately to the Security Response team using the incident hotline or approved reporting form. Tell the team when it happened and whether you downloaded a file or entered a password. Follow the response team's containment instructions; preserve relevant messages rather than forwarding dangerous links to coworkers. If credentials were entered, contact Security and IT for account protection and credential reset. Do not investigate the malicious site yourself. Sensitive employee and customer records must remain in approved access-controlled storage, with least-privilege sharing. Report suspected accidental disclosure promptly.
